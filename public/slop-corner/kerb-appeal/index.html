About the studio

Small games, same street

Kerb Appeal makes short games about the ordinary things that go wrong on an ordinary road. It is the same terrace every time. Parking is tight, the permits are confusing, and there is a primary school at the far end.

Everything runs in the browser. There is nothing to download and no account to make, and your scores stay on your device. The two daily games give everyone the same puzzle on the same day, so you can compare notes over the garden fence.
