Delete Your Data

Mercuro: Thermometer Logic Puzzles (Android) · Last updated: July 2026

The short version: Mercuro can be used without an account, and your puzzle progress lives on your device. You can delete everything yourself from inside the app, by uninstalling the app, or by emailing us. There is no separate account to close.

Delete Everything Inside the App

Open Mercuro, go to Settings, and use Delete Account & Data. This permanently removes your solved history, solve times, streaks, unlocks, settings, and in-progress puzzles from your device, disconnects any linked sign-in, and clears data synced to the cloud for your account. One-time purchases (Remove Ads, Mercuro Pro) can always be recovered later with Restore Purchases.

Cloud-Synced Progress

Optional sync uses Google Play Games Services saved games (Snapshots) tied to your own Google account. You can delete this at any time: disconnect inside Mercuro Settings, or open the Play Games app → your profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data and choose Mercuro.

Remove the App

Uninstalling Mercuro removes all local puzzle state and settings from your device. Cloud-synced progress, if you used it, is removed with the steps above.

Request Deletion by Email

If you would rather we handle it, email support@mercuro.app with the subject Delete my data from the address associated with your device or sign-in. We confirm and complete deletion of any data we can associate with you within 30 days.

What We Keep

Mercuro does not run a server for your game progress and does not build an account profile for you. Analytics are collected anonymously and are never linked to your identity, so they cannot be tied back to an individual for deletion; they exist only as aggregate, non-identifying counts.

Questions about deletion or privacy? Contact support@mercuro.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
