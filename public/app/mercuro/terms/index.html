Game Content

Mercuro provides thermometer logic puzzles, daily puzzles, level packs, hints, achievements, leaderboards, and unlockable themes. Puzzle availability, difficulty, and features may change over time as the app improves.

Accounts and Sync

Sign in with Apple is optional. If enabled, Mercuro uses Apple services such as iCloud Key-Value Store to recover solved progress, streaks, purchases, and unlocks. Current in-progress puzzle grids remain local to your device.

Purchases

Mercuro offers optional one-time in-app purchases processed by Apple: Remove Ads removes advertising permanently, and Mercuro Pro unlocks additional puzzle chapters and all themes (and is ad-free). Purchases apply to the Apple ID used to buy them and can be restored from Settings.

Ads and Hints

The free version shows a full-screen ad every third puzzle you start, and optional short ads for extra hints and for past days in the daily archive. Ads are provided by third-party ad networks and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Game Center

Mercuro uses Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. Game Center behavior is governed by Apple's terms and settings.
