Privacy Policy

Mercuro: Thermometer Logic Puzzles (Android) · Last updated: July 2026

The short version: Mercuro can be used without an account. Your active puzzle state lives on your device. Optional sync uses Google Play Games with your own Google account. The free version can show ads, one-time purchase removes them permanently.

Data We Store (On Your Device)

Mercuro stores your current puzzle state, solved puzzle history, solve times, streaks, settings, purchases, and unlock state locally on your device using Android's DataStore.

Current in-progress puzzle grids are local-only. If you uninstall the app, local-only in-progress grids are removed.

Mercuro does not operate a server for your game progress.

Google Play Games Sign-In and Cloud Sync

Signing in with Google Play Games Services is optional. If you connect it, Mercuro syncs recoverable progress, solved puzzles, solve times, streaks, purchases, hints-used counters, and unlock state, to a Google Play Games saved game (Snapshot) tied to your own Google account.

Mercuro does not receive your Google account password. You can disconnect Play Games sign-in inside Mercuro Settings.

Google Play Games Leaderboards and Achievements

Mercuro integrates with Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. Play Games can receive leaderboard scores and achievement progress, such as solved puzzle counts, streaks, and completion milestones. Leaderboards and achievements are governed by Google's own Play Games Services privacy terms and can be managed from the Play Games app.

Advertising

The free version of Mercuro can display ads served by Google AdMob. Where required by your region, Mercuro uses Google's User Messaging Platform to gather consent before ads are shown. If you don't consent to personalized advertising, only non-personalized ads are shown where supported.

AdMob may collect device identifiers, coarse location, and ad interaction information for ad serving and fraud prevention. Your puzzle progress, solve history, and grid state are never sent to Mercuro servers for ad targeting.

You can remove all ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

In-App Purchases

Mercuro offers optional one-time purchases, Remove Ads, and Mercuro Pro, which unlocks additional puzzles and all themes, processed entirely by Google through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Analytics

Mercuro uses Mixpanel to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Analytics are collected anonymously: Mercuro does not create an account profile for you, does not ask Mixpanel to identify you, and does not send personal information. Collected events describe gameplay and feature usage, for example puzzles started and solved, screens viewed, and settings changed. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Notifications

If you enable the Daily Reminder, Mercuro schedules a local notification on your device to remind you about the day's puzzles. Scheduling happens on your device and does not send notification content to a Mercuro server. You can turn reminders off in Settings.

Data Deletion

Deleting Mercuro removes local in-progress puzzle state and local settings from your device. Data synced through Google Play Games can be removed by disconnecting inside Mercuro Settings or from the Play Games app. See our Delete Your Data page for step-by-step instructions.

Children's Privacy

Mercuro is rated for general audiences and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
